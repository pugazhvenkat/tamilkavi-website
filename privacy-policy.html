3. Storage & Media Permissions Policy

3. JUSTIFICATION FOR STORAGE ACCESS & WHATSAPP STATUS SAVER

In strict compliance with Google Play Developer policies regarding Sensitive Permissions, Tamil Kavi requests READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE, WRITE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE, MANAGE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE, READ_MEDIA_IMAGES, and READ_MEDIA_VIDEO solely for the following explicit user-initiated functionalities:
